Exercise: Cost-Benefit Analysis

Scenario: Your company is deciding between two architectural approaches for a new e-commerce platform.

Option A: Monolithic Architecture

  • PRO Lower development cost (~$200K)
  • PRO Faster time-to-market (3 months)
  • CON Higher maintenance cost over time
  • CON Harder to scale

Option B: Microservices Architecture

  • CON Higher development cost (~$400K)
  • CON Slower time-to-market (6 months)
  • PRO Lower maintenance cost over time
  • PRO Easier to scale

Analysis Summary

Low growth startup: Monolith is likely better — lower cost, faster to market. Get revenue flowing first.
High growth startup: Microservices may be better — scalability pays off. The higher upfront cost is an investment in growth.
Hybrid approach: Start monolith, plan migration path. Best of both worlds for many cases. Ship fast, refactor strategically.

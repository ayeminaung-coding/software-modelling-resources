Economic Factors

💰

Development Costs

The price of constructing the software.

Affects project budget and profitability
🔧

Maintenance Costs

Ongoing costs that accumulate over time.

Can exceed initial development costs
📈

Return on Investment (ROI)

The income produced vs. cost of creating and maintaining.

Key decision metric for stakeholders
🏭

Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

Includes development, maintenance, training, hardware, infrastructure.

The full picture of what a system really costs
📚

Other Factors

Pricing model, target market, revenue streams, competitive advantage.

Business context that shapes architectural decisions

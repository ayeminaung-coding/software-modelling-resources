Case Study: Monolith to Microservices

Scenario: A mid-size SaaS company (50 developers, 200K users) considers migrating their monolithic application to microservices. The monolith has growing deployment bottlenecks and team coordination issues. CBA over a 3-year horizon:

☁ Option B: Migrate to Microservices

Migration Costs

Ongoing (Per Year)
Operations & platform$180K
Feature velocity gain-$100K

❌ CBA Verdict: Don't Migrate Now

At current scale, the microservices option costs 2x more over 3 years. The ROI only turns positive at ~Year 5 with continued growth.

✅ Better Approach: Strangler Fig

Extract 2-3 high-change services first. Re-evaluate after 12 months. This limits risk while testing the microservices approach.

Goal: Achieve high availability through fault handling

Availability Tactics
↓
Fault Detection

Know when something is wrong

Fault Recovery

Fix or work around the fault

Fault Prevention

Stop faults before they happen

Remember: You can't prevent all faults. Good availability comes from combining all three categories: detect faults quickly, recover gracefully, and prevent what you can.

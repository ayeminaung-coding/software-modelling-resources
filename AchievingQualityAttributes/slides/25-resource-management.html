Resource Management Tactics

Increase available resources or optimize their usage

Introduce Concurrency

  • Parallel processing
  • Thread pools
  • Async/await patterns
  • Event loops

Maintain Multiple Copies

  • Data replication
  • Read replicas
  • CDN caching
  • In-memory caches

Increase Resources

  • Vertical scaling (up)
  • Horizontal scaling (out)
  • Auto-scaling groups
  • Serverless functions

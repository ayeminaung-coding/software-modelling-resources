Discussion: Recovery Strategy Selection

When would you use each recovery strategy?

Consider: cost, recovery time, data consistency, and system criticality

Scenario A: E-commerce Checkout

Payment processing during Black Friday sale

Requirements:

  • Zero transaction loss
  • Sub-second failover
  • High traffic volume

Scenario B: Analytics Database

Business intelligence reporting system

Requirements:

  • Data from last hour OK to lose
  • Few minutes downtime acceptable
  • Cost-sensitive

Scenario C: Archive Storage

Historical document storage system

Requirements:

  • Rarely accessed
  • Hours of downtime OK
  • Minimize ongoing costs

Discuss with your neighbor: Which recovery tactic (Hot/Warm/Cold/Checkpoint) for each scenario?

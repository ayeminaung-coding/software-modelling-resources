Discussion: Performance Optimization Trade-offs

Scenario

Your e-commerce platform experiences 10x traffic spikes during flash sales. Current architecture handles normal load but crashes during peaks. You have limited budget and a 2-week deadline.

Option A: Aggressive Caching

  • Add Redis cache layer
  • Cache product catalog for 5 minutes
  • Pre-compute popular queries

Trade-off: May show stale prices/inventory

Option B: Rate Limiting + Queuing

  • Queue checkout requests
  • Limit concurrent users
  • Show "virtual waiting room"

Trade-off: Users wait, may lose sales

Discussion Questions

  1. Which option would you choose and why?
  2. What metrics would you use to measure success?
  3. How would you handle inventory accuracy during caching?
  4. What hybrid approach might combine benefits of both?

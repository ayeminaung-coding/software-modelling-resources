Discussion: Balancing Quality Attributes

Real-World Scenario

You are architecting a banking mobile app. The product owner wants it to be extremely secure, highly performant, and easy to use. The deadline is 6 months.

Trade-off Tensions

Security vs. Usability MFA adds friction
Security vs. Performance Encryption adds latency
Modifiability vs. Performance Abstraction has overhead
Availability vs. Cost Redundancy is expensive

Consider

  • Which quality attributes are non-negotiable?
  • Where can you accept "good enough"?
  • How do you communicate trade-offs to stakeholders?
  • What metrics will validate your decisions?

Discussion Questions

  1. For a banking app, which quality attribute would you prioritize highest? Why?
  2. How would you make MFA user-friendly without compromising security?
  3. What tactics would you combine to achieve multiple quality attributes?

Time: 5 minutes group discussion | Present your priorities to the class

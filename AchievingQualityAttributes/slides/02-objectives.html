Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

1. Understand Tactics

Define what architectural tactics are and explain their role in achieving quality attributes

2. Apply Tactics

Apply appropriate tactics for availability and modifiability requirements

3. Recognize Relationships

Distinguish between tactics and patterns, understanding how they relate

4. Design Systems

Design systems that achieve specific quality attribute requirements using tactics

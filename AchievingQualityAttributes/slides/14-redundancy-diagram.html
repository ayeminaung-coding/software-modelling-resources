Active Redundancy

  • Zero/near-zero downtime on failure
  • Higher resource cost (all running)
  • Use: Critical services, real-time systems

Passive Redundancy

  • Brief downtime during failover
  • Lower cost (standbys idle)
  • Use: Databases, session stores

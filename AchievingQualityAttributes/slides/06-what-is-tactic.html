What is a Tactic?

Tactic: A design decision that influences the achievement of a quality attribute response.

Key Characteristics

  • Atomic: A single, focused design decision
  • Purposeful: Targets a specific quality attribute
  • Reusable: Can be applied across different systems
  • Composable: Can combine with other tactics

Examples

Availability

"Use redundancy" - deploy multiple instances

Performance

"Introduce caching" - store frequently accessed data

Security

"Encrypt data" - protect information in transit/at rest

"Tactics are the building blocks from which design patterns are created."
- Bass, Clements & Kazman

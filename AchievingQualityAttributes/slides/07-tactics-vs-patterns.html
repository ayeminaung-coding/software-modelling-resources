Tactics vs. Patterns

Tactic

  • Single design decision
  • Focuses on one quality attribute
  • Like a single ingredient
  • Example: "Use redundancy"

Pattern

  • Collection of design decisions
  • Addresses multiple concerns
  • Like a complete recipe
  • Example: "Microservices architecture"

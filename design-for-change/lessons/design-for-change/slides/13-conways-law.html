Conway's Law

"The structure of a designed system is isomorphic to the organizational structure of those who built it"

— Melvin Conway, 1967

Key Implications

  • If a design decision depends on a decision made by another team/company, coordination is required when changes happen
  • Communication channels: email, meetings, Slack, shared documents, etc.
  • Dependencies between teams = dependencies between components

Design Principles Recap

Design for Change
┃ ┃ ┃ ┃
H

Information Hiding

Hide decisions likely to change behind stable interfaces (Parnas)

S

Separation of Concerns

Each module addresses one concern — changes stay contained

C

Low Coupling

Minimize dependencies between modules to reduce ripple effects

A

Anticipate Change

Identify likely change points and design flexibility around them

These principles are complementary — information hiding enables low coupling, separation of concerns guides what to hide, and anticipating change tells you where to apply them.

Why Change Matters

"Software that cannot be changed is dead"

1. No Longer Used

Users find alternatives when software cannot adapt to their evolving needs

2. No Further Development

System becomes obsolete as the world moves forward without it

3. Rising Maintenance Costs

Bugs accumulate without fixes, every change becomes increasingly expensive

Design consists of making decisions. What happens when these decisions change? How can we design software so that likely-to-change decisions are easier to change?

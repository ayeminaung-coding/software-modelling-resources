Managing Technical Debt

📝

Debt Register

Track all known debt items with location, impact, and estimated effort to fix. Make debt visible to the whole team.

📊

Prioritize: Impact x Effort

Focus on high-impact, low-effort debt first. Use a simple matrix to decide what to pay down next.

⚡

Boy Scout Rule

"Leave the code better than you found it." Small improvements on every change prevent debt from accumulating.

🛠

Dedicated Refactoring Sprints

Allocate regular time (e.g., 20% of each sprint) specifically for paying down larger debt items.

The goal is not zero debt — it's managed, deliberate debt with a plan to pay it back

Case Study: Shopify (BFCM)

Shopify handles Black Friday/Cyber Monday - one of the highest e-commerce traffic events globally, with extreme burst scaling requirements.

Scaling Strategies

  • Pod Autoscaling: Based on request queue
  • Edge Caching: Static content at CDN
  • Read Replicas: Massive read scaling
  • Queue Everything: Async checkout

Resiliency Measures

  • Load Shedding: Reject excess load
  • Checkout Queue: Fair ordering
  • Feature Flags: Disable non-critical
  • Pre-scaling: Hours before peak
Lesson: Predictable high-traffic events require pre-scaling, load testing, and graceful degradation strategies.

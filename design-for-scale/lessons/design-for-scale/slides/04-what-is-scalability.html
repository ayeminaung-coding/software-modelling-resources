What is Scalability?

Scalability is the capability of a system to handle a growing amount of work, or its potential to be enlarged to accommodate that growth.

Key Characteristics

  • Handle increased load
  • Maintain performance
  • Cost-effective growth
  • Minimal downtime

Why It Matters

  • Business growth support
  • User experience
  • Competitive advantage
  • Cost optimization
"Scalability is not about being big, it's about having the ability to become big."
- Werner Vogels, CTO Amazon
